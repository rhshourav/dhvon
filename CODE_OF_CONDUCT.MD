# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want DHVON to be a friendly, safe place to ask questions, report problems and contribute, whoever you are. We pledge to keep participation free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that help build an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps build a positive community:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving, and gracefully accepting, constructive feedback
- Owning mistakes, apologizing to anyone affected, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the whole community, not only for ourselves

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Harassment, in public or in private
- Publishing other people's private information, such as a home address or email, without their explicit permission
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take fair and appropriate action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain why when they can.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and the website, and whenever someone is officially representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the maintainer, [@rhshourav](https://github.com/rhshourav), using the contact details on their GitHub profile. If you'd rather not use those, open a private report at <https://github.com/rhshourav/dhvon/security/advisories/new> and title it “Conduct report”.

All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer will respect the privacy and safety of the person who reported.

## Enforcement guidelines

The maintainer will follow these steps when deciding the consequences for a violation.

### 1. Correction

**Impact:** Inappropriate language or other behavior judged unprofessional or unwelcome here.

**Consequence:** A private, written warning that explains the violation and why it was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Impact:** A violation through a single incident or a series of actions.

**Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited contact with those enforcing this Code, for a set period. Breaking these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

**Impact:** A serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from any kind of interaction or public communication with the project for a set period. No public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited contact with those enforcing this Code, is allowed during this period. Breaking these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

**Impact:** A pattern of violating this Code, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from any kind of public interaction within the project.
