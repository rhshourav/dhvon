Principles

Quiet by design. Loud where it counts.

Offline-first

Your library already lives on your phone. Playing it never needs the internet.

Ad-free

Nothing sits between you and the frame. No banners, no interruptions.

Scoped storage

Reads media through MediaStore and the system picker. No all-files access requested.

01 · Find

Find it.

A hero banner, filter chips and Recently Played put what you just watched one tap away.

02 · Resume

Resume it.

Exact progress is saved on-device. Tap once and you’re back at 48%.

03 · Play

Play it.

Speed, Quality and Audio sit under your thumb. Video or music, the same calm screen.

    Engine

    If it exists, it plays.

    Media3 with FFmpeg extensions. Pick how it decodes, per file and per moment.

    Hardware decoding through MediaCodec: the coolest, longest-lasting way to play.

    Themes

    Make it yours.

    Try it right here. This page wears the same four themes as the app.

    Nothing is decoration

    Every choice has a reason.

    Obsidian #08090D

    OLED pixels at near-zero light. Less battery, deeper contrast, and the video is always the brightest thing.

    Silver, then purple

    Silver is the logo’s metal. Purple only ever means “active”: the seekbar, the selected tab, this page’s top line.

    System font

    No font download, no layout shift. SF Pro on Apple devices, Roboto on Android.

    One easing curve

    Everything decelerates like a physical object, so motion feels caused, not played.

    Transform and opacity

    The only properties the GPU animates without re-layout. That’s how it holds 60 fps.

    Reduced motion

    If your OS asks for calm, every animation here yields.
